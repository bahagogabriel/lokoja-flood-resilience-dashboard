About this dashboard

This dashboard connects multi-event Sentinel-1 flood observations, a spatially validated Random Forest susceptibility model, exposure indicators and infrastructure screening across Lokoja LGA.

How to interpret the map

Susceptibility indicates relative flood-prone conditions. Intervention priority additionally considers population, buildings, infrastructure and observed 2024 flooding. It does not represent flood depth, annual probability, damage or a final engineering design.

Data and tools

Sentinel-1, JRC Global Surface Water, Dynamic World, HydroSHEDS, WorldPop, Google Open Buildings and OpenStreetMap. Analysis used Google Earth Engine, ArcGIS Pro, Python and scikit-learn.
