Project context

About this dashboard

This study examined where flooding has been observed in Lokoja, which areas have conditions associated with flooding, and where people, buildings and important routes may justify earlier resilience investigations. Satellite observations from four years were combined with a flood-susceptibility model and geographical planning evidence. This is not a live flood warning or a record of confirmed property damage.

How to read the four maps

Where to act compares planning priorities. Possible actions groups locations by suggested investigation or response themes. Flood-prone areas shows relative modelled flood susceptibility, not an annual chance of flooding. Past floods shows individual satellite-observed flood years. Map symbols for roads, facilities and crossings are supporting context; their presence does not prove damage.

Intervention priority additionally considers modelled residents, mapped buildings, infrastructure and the observed 2024 flood evidence. The mapped population and buildings are not counts of people or structures confirmed affected. Neither flood susceptibility nor the planning score represents water depth, an annual flood probability, measured loss or a final engineering design. Field checks and engineering assessment remain necessary.

Data and tools

Sentinel-1, JRC Global Surface Water, Dynamic World, HydroSHEDS, WorldPop, Google Open Buildings and OpenStreetMap. Analysis used Google Earth Engine, ArcGIS Pro, Python and scikit-learn.
